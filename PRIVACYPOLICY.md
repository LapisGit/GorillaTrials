# Privacy Policy for GorillaTrials

Last updated: 1/7/2026

GorillaTrials is a Gorilla Tag modification developed by Lapis. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, and how it is used.

By using the mod, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

## Information We Collect

When you use GorillaTrials, the following information may be collected and sent to our API servers:

- PlayFab Player ID (used to uniquely identify your Gorilla Tag account)

- IP Address (used for tracking bans, and to prevent abuse on servers)

This information is collected only for the purpose of account creation and authentication.

## How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used exclusively to:

- Create and manage your GorillaTrials account

- Prevent abuse and ensure fair use of the mod

Your data is never used for tracking, advertising, analytics, or profiling, and is never shared with third parties.

## Data Storage and Privacy

- All collected data is stored on servers not accessible to anyone except for the server administrators. (gizmogoat and Lapis)

- Your data is not distributed, sold, logged elsewhere, or stored outside of the secure server environment

- GorillaTrials does not knowingly collect personal information beyond what is required for basic functionality and security. No additional personal data (such as real names or emails) is collected through the mod.

## Data Retention

Your data is retained only for as long as necessary to operate GorillaTrials. If the service is discontinued, stored data will be deleted within a reasonable timeframe.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in an updated version of this document.
